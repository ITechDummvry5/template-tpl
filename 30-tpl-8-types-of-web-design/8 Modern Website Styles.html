How to enroll

Step 1: Pick your program

Open the program list and choose your course. Read the requirements beside it before you continue.

Step 2: Upload your documents

Take a clear photo of each document in good light. Use JPG or PDF, under 5 MB each.

Step 3: Wait for approval

We email you within 2 to 3 working days. Use the status checker below to see where you are.

Check your application
